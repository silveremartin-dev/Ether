# NASA Cassini & USGS Astrogeology: Titan Datasets

## 🪐 Datasets in this directory

### 1. `titan_cassini_geomorphology_lopes2019.png`
* **Title**: Global Geomorphologic Map of Titan
* **Authors**: Rosaly M. C. Lopes, Michael J. Malaska, Ashley M. Schoenfeld, Alice Le Gall, et al.
* **Publication**: *Nature Astronomy*, vol. 4, pp. 228–233 (2020), published online Nov 18, 2019.
* **DOI**: [10.1038/s41550-019-0917-4](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41550-019-0917-4)
* **Agency / Repository**: USGS Astrogeology Science Center / NASA Planetary Data System (PDS) Imaging Node.
* **Instruments**: Cassini Synthetic Aperture Radar (SAR), Visual and Infrared Mapping Spectrometer (VIMS), Imaging Science Subsystem (ISS).
* **Units**:
  - `Lakes`: Liquid methane and ethane seas (Kraken Mare, Ligeia Mare, Punga Mare, Ontario Lacus).
  - `Dunes`: Equatorial linear tholin organic dunes (Belet, Fensal, Shangri-La).
  - `Plains`: Undifferentiated organic plains / tholins.
  - `Hummocky`: Icy bedrock mountain massifs (Mithrim Montes, Xanadu).
  - `Craters`: Impact structures (Menrva, Selk, Sinlap).
  - `Labyrinth`: Dissected karst valley networks eroded by methane rain.

### 2. Titan Topographic Elevation (DEM) Specifications
* **Cassini Altimeter & SARTopo DEM Reference**: Corlies et al. (2017), *Titan's Topography and Shape at the End of the Cassini Mission*, Geophysical Research Letters, 44, 11,754–11,761. Lorenz et al. (2013), *A global topographic map of Titan*, Icarus, 225(1), 367–377.
* **Mean Radius**: $2574.73 \pm 0.09\text{ km}$.
* **Depression range**: Kraken Mare floor reaches $-1200\text{ m}$ relative to mean geoid.
* **Elevation maximum**: Mithrim Montes peak reaches $+2014\text{ m}$.
