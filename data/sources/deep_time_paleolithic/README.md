## 2. Core Paleoclimatic & Geophysical Datasets

### A. Marine Benthic Isotope Stack (LR04)
* **Citation**: Lisiecki, L. E., & Raymo, M. E. (2005). *A Pliocene-Pleistocene stack of 57 globally distributed benthic $\delta^{18}\text{O}$ records*. Paleoceanography, 20(1), PA1003.
* **Temporal Domain**: 0 to 5.3 Ma (covers the entirety of the 3 Ma Pleistocene and Late Pliocene cycles).
* **Applied In**: Temperature anomalies ($\Delta T$), eustatic sea-level fluctuations, and continental ice sheet extent.

### B. Long-Term Orbital Solutions (La2004)
* **Citation**: Laskar, J., Robutel, P., Joutel, F., Gastineau, M., Correia, A. C. M., & Levrard, B. (2004). *A long-term numerical solution for the insolation quantities of the Earth*. Astronomy & Astrophysics, 428(1), 261-285.
* **Temporal Domain**: -20 Ma to +10 Ma.
* **Applied In**: Milankovitch orbital forcing ($65^\circ\text{N}$ summer solstice insolation in $\text{W/m}^2$, orbital eccentricity $e$, and obliquity $\varepsilon$).

### C. Antarctic Ice Core Records (EPICA Dome C)
* **Citation**: Lüthi, D., et al. (2008). *High-resolution carbon dioxide concentration record 650,000–800,000 years before present*. Nature, 453, 379–382; Jouzel, J., et al. (2007). *Orbital and millennial Antarctic climate variability over the past 800,000 years*. Science, 317(5839), 793-796.
* **Temporal Domain**: -800,000 BP to present; extrapolated back to -3,000,000 BP via boron isotope ($\delta^{11}\text{B}$) proxies (Martínez-Botí et al., 2015).
* **Applied In**: Atmospheric $\text{CO}_2$ concentration ($180 - 280\text{ ppm}$ in glacial/interglacial cycles, up to $360 - 400\text{ ppm}$ in Mid-Pliocene).

* **Citations**:
