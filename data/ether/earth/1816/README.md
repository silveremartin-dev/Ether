# Epoch 1816 CE: The Year Without a Summer (Tambora Volcanic Winter)

## Geophysical & Climatic Provenance
* **Cataclysm**: April 1815 eruption of Mount Tambora (Sumbawa, Dutch East Indies, VEI 7), ejecting ~100-150 Tg of tephra and ~100 Tg of sulfate aerosol precursors into the global stratosphere.
* **Surface Temperature Shock**: Northern hemisphere summer anomalies of -1.5°C to -3.0°C; persistent mid-summer frosts and snowstorms in New England and Western Europe.
* **Agricultural Disruption**: Widespread crop failures across Europe, North America, and Yunnan (China), precipitating the last great subsistence crisis in the Western world.
* **Baseline Alignment**: Anomaly referenced to pre-industrial 1850-1900 climatology.
