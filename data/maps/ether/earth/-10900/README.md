# Cartographic & Cultural Tensor Provenance: Earth (-10,900 BP)

## 🌍 1. Overview & Geophysical Context
The epoch **-10,900 BP** (c. 8900 BCE) corresponds to the climax of the **Younger Dryas**, an abrupt northern hemisphere cold reversal triggered by catastrophic meltwater discharge from glacial Lake Agassiz into the North Atlantic, causing a critical weakening of the Atlantic Meridional Overturning Circulation (AMOC).

* **Eustatic Sea Level**: $-60\text{ m}$ below present-day datum (Doggerland, Sundaland, and Beringia continental shelves remain widely exposed).
* **Residual Ice Sheets**: Fennoscandian ice sheet re-advancing in Scandinavia; Keewatin and Quebec residual domes of the Laurentide ice sheet.
* **Human Dynamics**:
  * **Levant (Natufian Culture)**: Early sedentism in permanent hamlets (Ain Mallaha, Hayonim, Shubayqa), cereal storage pits/silos, and intensive harvesting of wild cereals using sickle blades with sickle gloss.
  * **North America (Clovis / Early Folsom Horizon)**: Specialized big-game hunters with fluted points confronting the rapid post-LGM megafaunal turnover.
  * **South America (Early Settlers)**: Verified early occupation at Monte Verde II (Chile), Guitarrero Cave (Peru), and Fell's Cave (Patagonia).
  * **Northern Eurasia & Africa**: Epigravettian/Azilian in Europe, LSA in South Africa and Nile Valley, early hunter pottery in Xianrendong (China), and incipient Jōmon in Japan.
  * **Strict Uninhabited Masks**: Antarctica, central Greenland ice sheet, Madagascar, and isolated oceanic islands are strictly uninhabited ($D = 0.0\text{ hab/km}^2$).
