# Earth Epoch 1324: Empire du Mali & Commerce Trans-Saharien (1324)

## 🌍 Overview
This directory contains the standardized 25-layer cartographic raster tensor suite and cliodynamic registries for Earth at epoch **1324 BP** (Empire du Mali & Commerce Trans-Saharien (1324)).

All raster layers are generated in equirectangular projection (Plate Carrée, EPSG:4326) at **2048x1024** resolution with bit-identical determinism.

## 🗺️ Standard Cartographic Layers (25 PNG Rasters)

### Physical & Climate Layers
* `earth_1324_elevation.png`: NOAA ETOPO 2022 / GEBCO Topography & Bathymetry.
* `earth_1324_biomes.png`: Coupled Holdridge-Whittaker Bioclimatic Ecology.
* `earth_1324_temperature.png`: WorldClim v2.1 Annual Mean Temperature (°C).
* `earth_1324_precipitation.png`: WorldClim v2.1 Annual Precipitation (mm/year).
