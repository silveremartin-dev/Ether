# Cartographic & Cultural Tensor Provenance: Earth (-6000 BP)

## 🌍 1. Overview & Geophysical Context
The epoch **-6000 BP** (c. 4000 BCE, Holocene Climatic Optimum / Atlantic Period) represents a pivotal evolutionary phase marked by the rise of the first proto-urban theocratic chiefdoms, the development of copper smelting metallurgy, and gravity-fed canal irrigation in Lower Mesopotamia.

* **Eustatic Sea Level**: $0\text{ m}$ (modern baseline reached; marine highstand in the Persian Gulf extends far inland, placing Eridu and Ur on coastal/lagoonal embayments).
* **Climate & Green Sahara Peak**:
  * Holocene thermal maximum (*Hypsithermal*) with global temperatures $+1.0\text{ to }+1.5^\circ\text{C}$ above pre-industrial levels.
  * The **African Humid Period** reaches peak vegetation coverage: vast interconnected waterways and savannas, with Lake Mega-Chad covering over $350,000\text{ km}^2$, connected to the Nile via Wadi Howar.
  * Continuous dense broadleaf deciduous forests across Western and Central Europe.
* **Major Archaeological & Sociological Hearths**:
  * **Mesopotamia (Late Ubaid Period)**: Monumental terraced temples at **Eridu** (Temple VII), large-scale canal irrigation, slow-wheel pottery, and emerging theocratic hierarchy.
  * **Europe (Vinča Chalcolithic & Atlantic Megaliths)**: Advanced copper smelting at **Pločnik** and **Vinča-Belo Brdo** (Serbia/Balkans), incised Vinča symbols; construction of Atlantic megalithic alignments and passage graves (Carnac, Bougon, Barnenez).
  * **East Asia (Yangshao & Hemudu Cultures)**: Painted pottery culture of **Yangshao** (Banpo, Miaodigou) in the Yellow River basin; intensive paddy rice cultivation and stilt architecture at **Hemudu** (Yangtze delta); Hongshan jade ritual complexes (Niuheliang).
  * **Predynastic Egypt**: **Badarian** and Merimde Beni Salama cultures mastering Nile inundation flood-recession farming.
  * **Indian Subcontinent**: Period III-IV of **Mehrgarh** and early Hakra ware culture (pre-Harappan).
  * **Americas**: Middle Archaic earthwork mound centers at Watson Brake (Louisiana); early maritime/agricultural foundations of Caral / Norte Chico (Peru).
  * **Strict Uninhabited Masks**: Antarctica, Madagascar, Iceland, New Zealand, and Remote Oceania are strictly uninhabited ($D = 0.0\text{ hab/km}^2$).
