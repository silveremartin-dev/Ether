# Earth Epoch 1800: Révolution Industrielle & Transition Charbonnière (1800)

## 🌍 Overview
This directory contains the standardized 25-layer cartographic raster tensor suite and cliodynamic registries for Earth at epoch **1800 BP** (Révolution Industrielle & Transition Charbonnière (1800)).

All raster layers are generated in equirectangular projection (Plate Carrée, EPSG:4326) at **2048x1024** resolution with bit-identical determinism.

## 🗺️ Standard Cartographic Layers (25 PNG Rasters)

### Physical & Climate Layers
* `earth_1800_elevation.png`: NOAA ETOPO 2022 / GEBCO Topography & Bathymetry.
* `earth_1800_biomes.png`: Coupled Holdridge-Whittaker Bioclimatic Ecology.
* `earth_1800_temperature.png`: WorldClim v2.1 Annual Mean Temperature (°C).
* `earth_1800_precipitation.png`: WorldClim v2.1 Annual Precipitation (mm/year).
* `earth_1800_seasonality.png`: Temperature Seasonality Amplitude (°C range).

### Cliodynamic & Cultural Tensors
* `earth_1800_density.png`: HYDE 3.4 / Seshat Human Demographic Density.
* `earth_1800_sovereignty.png`: Political Sovereignty & Territorial Polity Domains.
* `earth_1800_isogloss.png`: Ethnolinguistic Phyla & Language Families.
* `earth_1800_kinship.png`: Murdock D-PLACE Kinship & Social Organization Systems.
* `earth_1800_rituals.png`: Monumental Ritual Centers & Sacred Traditions.
* `earth_1800_technology.png`: Technology & Innovation Complexity Index.
* `earth_1800_institutional.png`: Seshat Institutional Hierarchy & State Capacity.
* `earth_1800_ecological.png`: Anthropogenic Ecological Footprint.
* `earth_1800_pathogen.png`: Epidemiological & Endemic Pathogen Load.
* `earth_1800_tradenetwork.png`: Commercial Trade Arteries, Emporia & Ports.

### Geological & Energy Resources
* `earth_1800_coal.png`: Coal Basins (USGS WoCQI).
* `earth_1800_oil.png`: Conventional & Unconventional Petroleum Plays.
* `earth_1800_gas.png`: Natural Gas Formations.
* `earth_1800_uranium.png`: Uranium Mineral Deposits (IAEA).
* `earth_1800_helium3.png`: Mantle Plume Helium-3 Sources.
* `earth_1800_iron_copper.png`: Iron & Copper Mineralization (USGS MRDS).
* `earth_1800_precious_metals.png`: Gold, Silver & Platinum Group Deposits.
* `earth_1800_rare_earths.png`: Critical Rare Earth Elements (REE).
* `earth_1800_geothermal.png`: Terrestrial Heat Flow & Geothermal Gradients (IHFC).
* `earth_1800_aquifers.png`: Deep Regional Groundwater Aquifers (UNESCO WHYMAP).
