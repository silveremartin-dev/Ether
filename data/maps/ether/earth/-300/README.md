# Earth Epoch -300: Empire Maurya & Civilisation de l'Indus-Gange (-300)

## 🌍 Overview
This directory contains the standardized 25-layer cartographic raster tensor suite and cliodynamic registries for Earth at epoch **-300 BP** (Empire Maurya & Civilisation de l'Indus-Gange (-300)).

All raster layers are generated in equirectangular projection (Plate Carrée, EPSG:4326) at **2048x1024** resolution with bit-identical determinism.

## 🗺️ Standard Cartographic Layers (25 PNG Rasters)

### Physical & Climate Layers
* `earth_-300_elevation.png`: NOAA ETOPO 2022 / GEBCO Topography & Bathymetry.
* `earth_-300_biomes.png`: Coupled Holdridge-Whittaker Bioclimatic Ecology.
* `earth_-300_temperature.png`: WorldClim v2.1 Annual Mean Temperature (°C).
* `earth_-300_precipitation.png`: WorldClim v2.1 Annual Precipitation (mm/year).
* `earth_-300_seasonality.png`: Temperature Seasonality Amplitude (°C range).

### Cliodynamic & Cultural Tensors
* `earth_-300_density.png`: HYDE 3.4 / Seshat Human Demographic Density.
* `earth_-300_sovereignty.png`: Political Sovereignty & Territorial Polity Domains.
* `earth_-300_isogloss.png`: Ethnolinguistic Phyla & Language Families.
* `earth_-300_kinship.png`: Murdock D-PLACE Kinship & Social Organization Systems.
* `earth_-300_rituals.png`: Monumental Ritual Centers & Sacred Traditions.
* `earth_-300_technology.png`: Technology & Innovation Complexity Index.
* `earth_-300_institutional.png`: Seshat Institutional Hierarchy & State Capacity.
* `earth_-300_ecological.png`: Anthropogenic Ecological Footprint.
* `earth_-300_pathogen.png`: Epidemiological & Endemic Pathogen Load.

### Geological & Energy Resources
* `earth_-300_coal.png`: Coal Basins (USGS WoCQI).
* `earth_-300_oil.png`: Conventional & Unconventional Petroleum Plays.
* `earth_-300_gas.png`: Natural Gas Formations.

* `earth_-300_helium3.png`: Mantle Plume Helium-3 Sources.
