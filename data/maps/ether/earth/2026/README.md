# Earth Epoch 2026: Business As Usual : Fossil Fuel Reliance & Warming (SSP5-8.5)

## 🌍 Overview
This directory contains the standardized 25-layer cartographic raster tensor suite and cliodynamic registries for Earth at epoch **2026 BP** (Business As Usual : Fossil Fuel Reliance & Warming (SSP5-8.5)).

All raster layers are generated in equirectangular projection (Plate Carrée, EPSG:4326) at **2048x1024** resolution with bit-identical determinism.

## 🗺️ Standard Cartographic Layers (25 PNG Rasters)

### Physical & Climate Layers
* `earth_2026_elevation.png`: NOAA ETOPO 2022 / GEBCO Topography & Bathymetry.
* `earth_2026_biomes.png`: Coupled Holdridge-Whittaker Bioclimatic Ecology.
* `earth_2026_temperature.png`: WorldClim v2.1 Annual Mean Temperature (°C).
* `earth_2026_precipitation.png`: WorldClim v2.1 Annual Precipitation (mm/year).
* `earth_2026_seasonality.png`: Temperature Seasonality Amplitude (°C range).

### Cliodynamic & Cultural Tensors
* `earth_2026_density.png`: HYDE 3.4 / Seshat Human Demographic Density.
* `earth_2026_sovereignty.png`: Political Sovereignty & Territorial Polity Domains.
* `earth_2026_isogloss.png`: Ethnolinguistic Phyla & Language Families.
* `earth_2026_kinship.png`: Murdock D-PLACE Kinship & Social Organization Systems.
* `earth_2026_rituals.png`: Monumental Ritual Centers & Sacred Traditions.
* `earth_2026_technology.png`: Technology & Innovation Complexity Index.
* `earth_2026_institutional.png`: Seshat Institutional Hierarchy & State Capacity.
* `earth_2026_ecological.png`: Anthropogenic Ecological Footprint.
* `earth_2026_pathogen.png`: Epidemiological & Endemic Pathogen Load.
* `earth_2026_tradenetwork.png`: Commercial Trade Arteries, Emporia & Ports.

### Geological & Energy Resources
* `earth_2026_coal.png`: Coal Basins (USGS WoCQI).
* `earth_2026_oil.png`: Conventional & Unconventional Petroleum Plays.
* `earth_2026_gas.png`: Natural Gas Formations.

* `earth_2026_helium3.png`: Mantle Plume Helium-3 Sources.
