* **Epoch**: $-25\,000\text{ BP}$ (Late Pleistocene, Gravettian / Pavlovian Climax, Beringian Standstill, LGM Inception)
* **Planet**: Earth (*Terre*)
* **Grid Resolution**: $2048 \times 1024$ Equirectangular ($0.175^\circ \times 0.175^\circ$ per pixel, $\approx 19.5\text{ km}$ at equator)

* **Key Anthropological Milestones**:
  1. Climax of Gravettian mammoth hunting culture (Venus figurines, fired clay ceramics, mammoth bone architecture).
  2. Genetic isolation of the ancestral Native American population in the ice-free Beringian refugium (**Beringian Standstill**).
  3. Total extinction of Neanderthals and Denisovans (only *Homo sapiens* survives).

### 2.1 The Beringian Standstill Hypothesis
* **Genomic Evidence**: Ancient genomic and mtDNA studies (Llamas et al. 2016, Raghavan et al. 2014, Moreno-Mayar et al. 2018) show that the founding lineage of Native Americans branched off from East Asian and Ancient North Eurasian (ANE / Mal'ta) populations around $\sim 25\,000\text{ BP}$ and remained isolated in unglaciated Beringia for several millennia.
* **Geographical Isolation**: Massive continental ice sheets (Laurentide to the east and Cordilleran to the south) blocked passage into unglaciated North America until post-LGM deglaciation ($\sim 16\,000 - 14\,000\text{ BP}$).
* **Demographic Enforcement**: All of North and South America south of the ice sheets has a **strict population density of $0.0\text{ hab/km}^2$**.
