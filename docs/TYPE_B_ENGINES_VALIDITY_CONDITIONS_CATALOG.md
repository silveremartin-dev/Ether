## 1. Epistemic Foundation & Strict 1-to-1 Code Bijection Invariant

Unlike **Tier 1 (Type A)** biophysical engines—which encode universal, invariant conservation laws of physics, chemistry, thermodynamics, and cross-taxa bioenergetics (First and Second Laws of Thermodynamics, Kooijman DEB reserve-structure dynamics, Brown et al. MTE allometry, Sterner & Elser C:N:P stoichiometry, Stommel thermohaline circulation, Howard-Kerby stream power erosion) that apply unconditionally across all planets and epochs—**Tier 2 (Type B)** engines formalize **context-dependent sociological, institutional, technological, capital-intensive, or geopolitical mechanisms**.

Per **`AGENTS.md §8.2, §11.3, §12.4, and §13`**:
1. **Strict 1-to-1 Bijection Between Catalog, UI Checkboxes, and Java Classes**:
   Every single engine documented in this catalog and exposed in `ScenarioEngineCatalog.getOptionalEngines()` corresponds **strictly 1-to-1** to an existing, executable Java class in `org.ether.society.engines.tier2.theories`, `org.ether.society.engines.tier2.historical`, or optional anthropogenic modules in `org.ether.society.engines.tier1` (**86 verified Type B engines in total**).
   - Permanent Tier 1 biophysical conservation laws (`DeforestationErosionEngine`, `BioMolecularEpidemiologyEngine`, `EntropicMetalDissipationEngine`, `MegafaunaEcosystemEngine`) are documented in `ScenarioEngineCatalog.getCoreEngines()` and execute unconditionally in Tier 1 passes.
   - Deprecated speculative or duplicate alias classes (`PsychohistoryPureEngine`, `KardashevPureEngine`, `TechnologicalSingularityEngine`, `AiAutonomousRegulationPureEngine`, `AutoRegulationPureEngine`, `FertileCrescentSalinizationEngine`) have been permanently removed from the codebase and UI catalogs.
2. **Full Deep-Time to Multi-Century Prospective Horizon Coverage ($-3\,000\,000\text{ BP}$ to $2200\text{ CE}$)**:
   In accordance with `AGENTS.md §1, §8.2, and §13`, Ether spans the complete chronological continuum from **$-3\,000\,000\text{ BP}$** (Plio-Pleistocene / Lower Paleolithic hominin emergence) through the empirical historical baseline ($2025\text{ CE}$) and into the **multi-century prospective horizon up to $2200\text{ CE}$** (`SSP1`–`SSP5`, `ETHER_CALIBRATED`, and off-Earth ISRU/terraforming trajectories). All engines valid in the modern era remain active across prospective runs up to **$2200\text{ CE}$**.
3. **Prohibition of Artificial Cross-Epoch Overfitting**:
   A Type B engine must **never** be forced to operate outside its historical or structural domain via hidden internal sigmoid gates (`if (kpc < 500) return;`), calendar hacks (`if (year < 1800)`), or artificial parameter distortions.
4. **Default Unchecking Outside Validity Domain (`false` by Default)**:
   When a scenario is initialized for a planet $P$ and start year $t_0$ via `AutoEpochScenarioGenerator` (`AutoEpochScenarioHelper.calibrateTypeBEngines(planetKey, year)`), any Type B engine whose spatiotemporal, technological, capital, or geopolitical prerequisites are not met at $(P, t_0)$ is **explicitly unchecked (`false`) by default**.

## 9. Extraterrestrial & Exoplanetary Modeling Directives (`!isEarth`)

In accordance with `AGENTS.md §1` (Delineation of Canonical Earth History vs. Heuristic Exploratory Sandbox) and `AGENTS.md §3` (Strict Ban on Arbitrary Heuristic Noise):
1. **Physical & Life-Support Realism**: Extraterrestrial settlements (whether on Moon, Mars, Venusian aerostats, Mercurian Caloris mines, Titan cryo-seas, or exoplanets like Super-Earth or Eyeball worlds) operate in sealed, artificial environments (`HabitatType != NONE`). Open-air terrestrial biological dynamics (open soil nitrogen-phosphorus weathering, rain-fed cereal farming, oceanic fisheries, wild megafauna hunting) do not exist.
2. **The 15 Valid Non-Earth Engines**:
   - **Hominin Bio-Social Constants (3)**: Human crews remain biological organisms governed by Hamilton's kin selection (`KinSelectionHamiltonEngine`), lexical glottochronological evolution (`LanguageLinguisticEngine`), and cultural trait diffusion (`CulturalSociologyEngine`).
   - **Enclosed Settlement & Urban Scaling (4)**: Sealed domes and subterranean lava tubes function as high-density urban clusters subject to Zipfian hierarchy (`SpatialCityFractalEngine`), Bettencourt-West allometry (`WestBettencourtAllometryEngine`), Tinbergen inter-base trade routing (`TradeNetworkRouter`), and administrative overhead collapse under life-support maintenance costs (`TainterComplexityCollapseEngine`).
   - **Advanced Industrial Technosphere & Automation (7)**: Extreme survival margins require modular capital recombination (`ArthurCombinatorialTechnologyEngine`), thermodynamic exergy conversion (`KummelAyresExergyEngine`), input-output Leontief matrices (`PhysicalLeontiefInputOutputEngine`), just-in-time supply buffers (`PhysicalSupplyChainEngine`), PID feedback automation (`WienerCyberneticHomeostasisEngine`), Shannon telecommunication channels (`InformationEntropyEngine`), and fission/fusion reactor safety (`NuclearSafetyRadiotoxicityEngine`).
   - **In-Situ Resource Utilization (1)**: Closed-loop volatile, water-ice, and mineral extraction (`IsruAutarkyAndSpaceColonizationEngine`).
3. **Explicit Prohibition of Fallback Reliance**:
   All non-Earth scenario presets must explicitly declare all 86 Type-B engine keys in their serialized JSON representation (`typeBEngineStates`), setting the 15 valid engines to `true` and the 71 invalid terrestrial engines to `false`. No scenario should rely on ambient code fallback defaults.
